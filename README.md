Media player for your own M3U & Xtream playlists on Android TV & Google TV


MxIPTV Player is a fast, lightweight media player designed specifically for Android TV and Google TV devices. 

Manage and stream your personal media playlists with an intuitive interface optimized for standard TV remote controls (D-Pad navigation).

KEY FEATURES:
• Designed for TV: Clean, leanback-optimized user interface for Android TV, Google TV, and TV boxes.
• Easy Playlist Setup: Connect your playlists quickly via our web management portal using your device ID / MAC address.
• Playlist Support: Compatible with standard M3U playlists and Xtream Codes API.
• Live TV & EPG: Electronic Program Guide (EPG) support for easy channel navigation.
• Catch-up Support: Easily replay past broadcasts and missed shows directly from the EPG grid (requires provider support).
• VOD & Series: Organized browsing for video-on-demand content and TV series included in your playlist.
• Advanced Player: Fast channel switching, and hardware-accelerated playback.
• Remote Friendly: Full navigation control using standard TV remotes without requiring a mouse or touchscreen.

HOW TO USE:
1. Open the app on your Android TV device to retrieve your unique Device ID / MAC address.
2. Visit https://mxpl.eu/ in any web browser on your phone or computer.
3. Enter your Device ID and add your own M3U playlist URL or Xtream Codes login credentials.
4. Return to the app and reload to start watching your personal media.

TRIAL & ACTIVATION:
MxIPTV Player offers a 7-day free trial so you can test all features on your device. After the trial period, full access can be unlocked via a one-time in-app purchase.
